
Maskified Origins
=======

Making a few origins to include in my modpack, partially as a tribute to the friends who helped me make it.

What Origins are included?
============
Two different dragon types, Abyssal and Tropical Storm. 

Then to put brief the abilities/powers of each Origin:

Abyssal Dragon
===
Fluid-like Physiology: Allows you to phase through blocks at the cost of hunger.

Void Poison: Apply a withering venom on hit.

Claws: Additional unarmed damage when hands are empty.

Hydrodynamic: Double speed in water.

Alien Anatomy: Immune to ALL potion effects (good and bad) and require no oxygen.

Cold-Adapted: Immune to cold and freeze damage.

Voidborne: Endermen become neutral, but will defend themselves.

Void Body: Cannot use armor stronger than gold.

Void Hands: Incapable of using a shield.

Dragon-Sized: 3 blocks tall and 2 blocks wide hitbox.

Big Appetite: Hunger drains 50% faster.

Carnivore: Can only eat meat.

Flammable: Take double fire damage from all sources.

Improved Regeneration: Applies Regeneration I indefinitely.

Durable: Gives you the armor value of a full set of iron armor.

Scary!: Villagers flee and refuse to trade, Iron Golems will target you, and lesser monsters will keep their distance to some degree.

Tropical Storm Dragon
===
Summon Lightning: Bring down a lightning bolt at the cost of hunger.

Venom: Poison I applied on hit.

Claws: Additional unarmed damage when hands are empty.

Hydrodynamic: Double speed in water.

Cold-Blooded: Applies weakness, mining fatigue, and slowness when in cold biomes (or near snow and ice).

Dragon-Sized: 3 blocks tall and 2 blocks wide hitbox.

Dragon Wings: Built-in Elytra.

Diver: Twice the oxygen when below water.

Big Appetite: Hunger drains 50% faster.

Agile: Cannot wear armor above chainmail in strength.

Scary!: Villagers flee and refuse to trade, Iron Golems will target you, and lesser monsters will keep their distance to some degree.

Avali
===
Summon Drone: Summons a drone to defend you.

Snow Walking: You do not sink into powder snow, unless you want to.

Smol: Fits into 1x1 blocks.

Cold-Adaapted: Immune to cold and freeze damage.

Ammonia Physiology: Makes positive potion effects useless and cannot eat sussy stews at all.

Standard Augments: Bigger jump height, slower fall speed, take 50% less blast damage, as well as taking no fall damage.

Innate Night-Vision: Night vision, duh.

Wings for Avalon: Built-in Elytra.

Improved Regeneration: Gives you Regeneration I indefinitely.

Flammable: Take double fire damage from all sources.

Less Health: Half the hearts as normal.
